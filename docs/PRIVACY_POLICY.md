# Privacy Policy for 21-S

**Effective Date:** January 15, 2026

## Introduction

Welcome to **21-S (21 Days to Social Mastery)** ("we," "our," or "us"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application (the "App").

By using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

## 1. Information We Collect

### A. Blockchain Data
21-S is a decentralized application (dApp) running on the Solana blockchain.
- **Public Transactions:** When you interact with the App (e.g., claiming a badge, making a transfer), transactions are broadcast to the Solana blockchain. These transactions are public and permanent.
- **Wallet Address:** We do not store your private keys. We only read your public wallet address to identify your account and display your assets.

### B. Device and Usage Data
We may collect non-personal information about your device and how you use the App to improve performance and user experience:
- **Device Information:** Model, operating system version, and unique device identifiers (if applicable for analytics, though we prioritize privacy).
- **Usage Data:** Screen navigation paths (as tracked locally or via anonymous analytics) to understand user flows.

### C. Local Storage
We use local storage on your device (`AsyncStorage`) to persist your app preferences and local progress state. This data stays on your device and is not transmitted to our servers unless explicitly required for a feature (e.g., syncing).

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:
- Provide and maintain the App's functionality (e.g., verifying challenge completion).
- Facilitate blockchain interactions (e.g., constructing transactions for you to sign).
- Improve the App's performance and fix bugs.

## 3. Third-Party Services

We may use third-party services that collect, monitor, and analyze this type of information to increase our App's functionality. These third-party service providers have their own privacy policies addressing how they use such information.
- **Solana RPC Providers:** To communicate with the blockchain.
- **Wallet Providers:** The wallet app you use (e.g., Phantom, Solflare) handles your keys and signing.

## 4. Security

- **Non-Custodial:** We are a non-custodial application. We never have access to your private keys or seed phrases.
- **Data Security:** We strive to use commercially acceptable means to protect your data, but remember that no method of transmission over the internet or method of electronic storage is 100% secure.

## 5. Children's Privacy

Our App is not intended for use by children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13.

## 6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page. You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes.

## 7. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us via the project repository or support channels.
